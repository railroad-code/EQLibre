# QCY-T13 ANC presets

These presets are based on publicly available tuning information associated with the official QCY app and are intended as a practical reference for personal audio tuning.

This page contains the device-specific tuning presets for the QCY-T13 ANC. These profiles are tailored to the hardware and can be used as a starting point for tuning with Easy Effects.

The goal is to provide a reference configuration that can be compared, adjusted, and reused depending on your source, ear profile, and listening environment.

> Disclaimer: These presets are provided as reference configurations for experimentation and personal listening. They are not guaranteed to fit every unit or environment. Adjust the values according to your equipment and listening taste.
